# 7.5.3.48 通用梳齿偏移序号信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
CombOffset-1 ::= ENUMERATED {off0, off1, off2, off3}
CombOffset-2 ::= ENUMERATED {off0, off1, off2, off3, off4, off5, off6, off7}
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>针对某种信息或信号的梳齿集合信息 CombInfo和梳齿偏移序号信息 CombOffset,当子载波编号满足$\mathrm{mod}(\#\mathrm{subcarrier},\mathrm{CombInfo})=\mathrm{mod}(\mathrm{CombOffset},\mathrm{CombInfo})$时,该子载波发送该信息或信号。</p>

:::
