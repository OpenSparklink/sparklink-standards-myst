# 7.5.3.75 RSSI信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
RSSI-Value ::= INTEGER (0..76)
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>RSSI反馈的范围为$-100\mathrm{dBm}\sim -25\mathrm{dBm}$,反馈粒度为1dB。反馈信令的值与 RSSI测量值的映射关系如表33所示。</p>

:::
