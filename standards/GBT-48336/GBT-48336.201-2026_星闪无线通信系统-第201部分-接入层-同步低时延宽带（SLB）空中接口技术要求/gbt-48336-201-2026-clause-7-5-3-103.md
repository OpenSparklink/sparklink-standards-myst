# 7.5.3.103 测量结果信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
MeasResults ::= SEQUENCE {
    rsrp    SEQUENCE (SIZE(1..16)) OF RSRP-Measure  OPTIONAL,
    rsrq    SEQUENCE (SIZE(1..16)) OF RSRQ-Measure  OPTIONAL,
    sinr    SEQUENCE (SIZE(1..16)) OF SINR-Measure  OPTIONAL,
    rssi    SEQUENCE (SIZE(77)) OF UINT16   OPTIONAL,
    timeSync    SEQUENCE (SIZE(1..16)) OF TimeDomainSync-Measure OPTIONAL,
    frequencySync   SEQUENCE (SIZE(1..16)) OF FrequencySync-Measure OPTIONAL,
    syncDomainGroupList SEQUENCE (SIZE(1..4)) OF SyncDomainGroupInfo    OPTIONAL,
    channelBusyRatio    ChannelBusyRatioMeasure OPTIONAL,
    a1Indicator BoolType    OPTIONAL,
    a2Indicator BoolType    OPTIONAL,
    ...
}
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>测量结果信息包含rsrp、rsrq、sinr、rssi、时间同步信息、频率同步信息、同步通信域集合信息以及信道忙占比测量量的具体值,或者是载波上的测量量满足 A1或者 A2时间事件触发条件的指示信息,其中当满足触发条件时a1Indicator或者a2Indicator的值为1。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>rssi是77个RSSI测量计数结果,其中,T节点在配置的测量时间内持续进行RSSI测量,第一个计数结果指示 RSSI测量结果落在表33中第一个区间的次数,第二个计数结果指示 RSSI测量结果落在表33中第二个区间的次数,依次类推。</p>

:::
