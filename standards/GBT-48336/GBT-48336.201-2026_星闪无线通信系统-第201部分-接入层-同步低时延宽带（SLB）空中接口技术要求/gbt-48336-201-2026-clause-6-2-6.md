# 6.2.6 信道编码

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1)=
## 6.2.6.1 极化码

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-1)=
### 6.2.6.1.1 码块分段及码块循环冗余校验级联

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>码块分段的输入比特序列表示为$b_{0}$,$b_{1}$,$b_{2}$,$b_{3},\ldots ,b_{B-1}$,其中$B\gt 0$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>对于第一类数据信息和第二类数据信息,根据6.2.5.2.2、6.2.5.3.2的处理方法确定码块数量$C$。如果码块数量$C$ 大于1,输入比特序列需要分段并对每个分段增加$L=16$的循环冗余校验序列。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>If $C==1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$L=0$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$B'=B$</p>
<p>else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$L=16$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$B'=B+C\cdot L$</p>
<p>$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>码块分段的输出比特序列表示为$c_{r(0)}$,$c_{r(1)},\ldots ,c_{r(K_{r}-1)}$,其中$0\leq r\lt C$ 是码块号$,K_{r}=K$ 是码块$r$的比特数$,K$ 的值为$K=B'/C.$</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>比特序列$c_{rk}$的生成方法为:</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>$s=0$;</p>
<p>for $r=0$ to $C-1$</p>
<p>&#8195;for $k=0$ to $K-L-1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$c_{r(k)}=b_{s}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$s=s+1$;</p>
<p>&#8195;$\;\text{end for}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;if $C\gt 1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;for $k=K-L$ to $K-1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$c_{r(k)}=p_{r(k+L-K)}$;</p>

:::
:::{div}
:class: algorithm-line

<p>endfor</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: algorithm-line

<p>endif</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: algorithm-line

<p>endfor</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>其中,循环冗余校验比特$p_{r(0)}$,$p_{r(1)},\ldots ,p_{r(L-1)}$是根据6.5.1的处理方法,针对序列$c_{r(0)}$,$c_{r(1)},\ldots ,$$c_{r(K-L-1)}$使用循环冗余校验生成多项式$g_{\mathrm{CRC}16}(D)$计算生成。</p>

:::

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-2)=
### 6.2.6.1.2 编码流程

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-2-1)=
#### 6.2.6.1.2.1 码长确定

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>信道编码方案采用 Polar码,输入到信道编码的码块的比特序列表示为$:c_{0}$,$c_{1}$,$c_{2}$,$c_{3},\ldots ,c_{K-1}$,其中$K$ 为码块的信息比特数量。经过信道编码后,输出的编码后比特序列表示为$:d_{0}$,$d_{1}$,$d_{2}$,$d_{3},\ldots ,$$d_{N-1}$,其中$N=2^{n}$,$n$ 的数值通过下述过程确定。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>记第一次传输速率匹配后的输出比特序列长度为 $E_0$，$n_1=[\log_2 E_0]$，$n=\max\{\min\{n_1,n_{\mathrm{max}}\},n_{\mathrm{min}}\}$，$n_{\mathrm{min}}=5$，其中 $n_{\mathrm{max}}$ 根据 6.2.6.1.5.1 和 6.2.6.1.6.1 确定。最小码长 32，最大码长取决于业务类型。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>Polar码应满足配置$K+n^{\mathrm{wm}}_{\mathrm{PC}}\lt E_{0}$。</p>

:::

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-2-2)=
#### 6.2.6.1.2.2 可靠度排序序列

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>按照附录 C确定极化码的可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}^{N_{\mathrm{max}}-1}_{0}=\{Q^{N_{\mathrm{max}}}_{0},Q^{N_{\mathrm{max}}}_{1},\ldots ,Q^{N_{\mathrm{max}}}_{N_{\mathrm{max}}-1}\}$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>表C.1确定了1024长的极化码可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}_{0}^{1023}$,其中$0\leq Q_{i}^{1024}\leq 1023$表示1024长极化码编码前的比特索引。$\mathbf{Q}_{0}^{1023}$按可靠度递增顺序排列$W(Q_{0}^{1024})\lt W(Q^{1024}_{1})\lt \ldots \lt W(Q^{1024}_{1023}),$其中$W$$(Q_{i}^{1024})$表示比特索引$Q_{i}^{1024}$的可靠性度量值。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>基于$\mathbf{Q}_{0}^{1023}$按以下方式确定可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}^{N_{\mathrm{max}}-1}_{0}$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>$N=1024$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;While $(N\lt N_{\mathrm{max}})$</p>
<p>$\boldsymbol Q_0^{2N-1}=\boldsymbol Q_0^{N-1}\otimes[1\quad1]$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{index}0=0$,$\mathrm{index}1=0$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;for $i=0$ to $2N-1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $(Q_{i}^{2N}\lt N/2)$</p>
<p>$Q_{i}^{2N}=Q^{N}_{\mathrm{index}0}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{index}0=\mathrm{index}0+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;else</p>
<p>$Q_{i}^{2N}=Q^{N}_{\mathrm{index}1}+N$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{index}1=\mathrm{index}1+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end for}\;$</p>
<p>$N=N\times 2$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end while}\;$</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>其中$\mathbf{Q}^{N-1}_{0}\otimes [11]$表示序列$\mathbf{Q}^{N-1}_{0}$与矩阵[11]的克罗内克积。</p>

:::

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-2-3)=
#### 6.2.6.1.2.3 极化编码

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>极化码的可靠度排序序列表示为$\mathbf{Q}^{4095}_{0}=\{Q^{4096}_{0},Q^{4096}_{1},\ldots ,Q^{4096}_{4095}\}$,由6.2.6.1.2.2中描述的极化码可靠度序列扩展方式确定,其中$0\leq Q_{i}^{4096}\leq 4095$是极化码编码前的比特索引$,i=0$,1,…,4095。可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}^{4095}_{0}$ 是按照可靠性度量值的升序排列产生$W(Q^{4096}_{0})\lt W(Q^{4096}_{1})\lt \ldots \lt W(Q^{4096}_{4095})$,其中$W$$(Q_{i}^{4096})$是比特索引$Q_{i}^{4096}$的可靠性度量值。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>任意一个码长为$N$ 的码块都使用相同的可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}^{N-1}_{0}=\{Q^{N}_{0},Q^{N}_{1},Q^{N}_{2},\ldots ,Q^{N}_{N-1}\}$。长为$N$ 的极化序列$\mathbf{Q}^{N-1}_{0}$是可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}^{4095}_{0}$ 的子集,通过在可靠度排序序列$\mathbf{Q}^{4095}_{0}$ 中选取比特索引小于$N$ 的序列,并按照可靠性度量值的升序排列产生$W(Q^{N}_{0})\lt W(Q^{N}_{1})\lt \ldots \lt W(Q^{N}_{N-1})$,二者构成嵌套关系。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>将可靠度排序序列 $\boldsymbol Q_0^{N-1}$ 中非冻结比特的索引组成的集合表示为 $\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$，将可靠度排序序列 $\boldsymbol Q_0^{N-1}$ 中冻结比特的索引组成的集合表示为 $\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_F^N$，$\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 和 $\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_F^N$ 根据 6.2.6.1.3.2 确定，$\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 包含的元素个数为 $|\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N|=K+n_{PC}$，$\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_F^N$ 包含的元素个数为 $|\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_F^N|=N-|\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N|$，$n_{\mathrm{PC}}$ 是奇偶校验比特的个数。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>将 $\boldsymbol G_N=(\boldsymbol G_2)^{\otimes n}$ 表示为矩阵 $\boldsymbol G_2$ 的 $n$ 次克罗内克积，其中 $\boldsymbol G_2=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{bmatrix}$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>对于比特索引$j$,$j=0$,$1,\ldots ,N-1$,将$\mathbf{g}_{j}$ 表示为矩阵$\mathbf{G}_{N}$ 的第$j$ 行,将$w(\mathbf{g}_{j})$表示为$\mathbf{g}_{j}$的重量,也就是$\mathbf{g}_{j}$中1的个数。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>将奇偶校验比特的比特索引组成的集合表示为 $\boldsymbol Q_{PC}^N$，$\boldsymbol Q_{PC}^N$ 包含的元素的个数为 $|\boldsymbol Q_{PC}^N|=n_{\mathrm{PC}}$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>$(n_{\mathrm{PC}}-n_{\mathrm{PC}}^{\mathrm{wm}})$ 个校验比特放置在 $\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 集合中 $(n_{\mathrm{PC}}-n_{\mathrm{PC}}^{\mathrm{wm}})$ 个可靠性度量值最小的比特索引上。剩余的 $n_{\mathrm{PC}}^{\mathrm{wm}}$ 个校验比特放置在 $\widetilde{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 中行重量最小的比特索引上，其中 $\widetilde{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 是在 $\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 中个可靠性度量值最大的 $|\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N|-n_{\mathrm{PC}}$ 个比特索引。如果在 $\widetilde{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 中相同的最小行重的比特索引多于 $n_{\mathrm{PC}}^{\mathrm{wm}}$ 个，则剩余的 $n_{\mathrm{PC}}^{\mathrm{wm}}$ 放置在 $\widetilde{\boldsymbol Q}_I^N$ 中具有最小行重的 $n_{\mathrm{PC}}^{\mathrm{wm}}$ 个最大可靠性度量值对应的比特索引上。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>通过下面的方法产生极化码编码器的输入序列$u=[u_{0},u_{1},u_{2},\ldots ,u_{N-1}]$:</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>$k=0$;</p>
<p>$l=0$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;if $n_{PC}\gt 0$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$y_{0}=0$;$y_{1}=0$;$y_{2}=0$;$y_{3}=0$;$y_{4}=0$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;for $n=0$ to $N-1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$y_{t}=y_{0}$;$y_{0}=y_{1}$;$y_{1}=y_{2}$;$y_{2}=y_{3}$;$y_{3}=y_{4}$;$y_{4}=y_{t}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $n\in \overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{N}_{I}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $n\in \mathbf{Q}^{N}_{PC}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$u_{n}=y_{0}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$u_{n}=c_{k}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$k=k+1$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$y_{0}=y_{0}\otimes u_{n}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$u_{n}=0$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end for}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;for $n=0$ to $N-1$</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $n\in \overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{N}_{I}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$u_{n}=c_{k}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$k=k+1$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$u_{n}=0$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end for}\;$</p>
<p>$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>编码器的输出比特为$\mathbf{d}=[d_{0},d_{1},d_{2},\ldots ,d_{N-1}]$,$\mathbf{d}=\mathbf{uG}_{N}$ ,其中矩阵在 GF(2)中运算。</p>

:::

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-3)=
### 6.2.6.1.3 速率匹配

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>每个码块的速率匹配包括极化比特索引确定、信道编码比特序列生成、初传发送信道编码比特选择、重传发送信道编码比特选择和信道交织。速率匹配的输入比特序列表示为$d_{0}$,$d_{1}$,$d_{2},\ldots ,d_{N-1}$,速率匹配的输出比特序列表示为$f_{0}$,$f_{1}$,$f_{2},\ldots ,f_{E-1}$。</p>

:::

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-3-1)=
#### 6.2.6.1.3.1 极化比特索引确定

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>根据第一次传输速率匹配后的输出比特序列长度$E_{0}$,母码长度 N,信息数量$K''$确定$T_{I1}$,其中,</p>

:::

```{math}
\begin{aligned}K'&=K+n_{\mathrm{PC}}\\&\text{if }(E_0\geq N)\parallel(K'/E_0>3/4)\\T_{\mathrm{I1}}&=K';\\&\text{else if }(K/E_0)\leq7/16\quad\text{//该条件下采用打孔}\\&\quad\text{if }(E_0/N)<5/8\\T_{\mathrm{I1}}&=\left[\frac{11\times E_0\times K'}{2\times N}-\frac{43\times K'}{16}\right]\\&\quad\text{else if }(E_0/N)<3/4\\T_{\mathrm{I1}}&=\left[\frac{5\times E_0\times K'}{4\times N}-\frac{K'}{32}\right]\\&\quad\text{else}\\T_{\mathrm{I1}}&=\left[\frac{3\times E_0\times K'}{8\times N}+\frac{5\times K'}8\right]\\&\quad\text{end if}\qquad\text{// }(E_0/N)<5/8\\&\text{else}\qquad\text{//该条件下采用缩短}\\&\quad\text{if }(E_0/N)<9/16\\T_{\mathrm{I1}}&=\left[\frac{9\times K'}8-\frac{E_0\times K'}{4\times N}\right]\\&\quad\text{else}\\T_{\mathrm{I1}}&=\left[\frac{31\times K'}{32}+\frac{E_0\times K'}{32\times N}\right]\\&\quad\text{end if}\\&\text{end if}\end{aligned}
```

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>非冻结比特的索引值组成的集合表示为$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{N}_{I}$ ,冻结比特的索引值组成的集合表示为$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{N}_{F}$ ,$K$、$n_{PC}$ 和$\mathbf{Q}^{N-1}_{0}$在6.2.6.1.2.2中规定。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F},\mathrm{tmp}}=\phi$;$//\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F},\mathrm{tmp}}$是根据信道速率适配确定的冻结位</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;if $E_{0}\lt N$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $K/E_{0}\leq 7/16//$打孔</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $N\leq 256//$预冻结</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F},\mathrm{tmp}}=\{0,1,\ldots [3N/4-E_{0}/2]-1\}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F},\mathrm{tmp}}=\{0,1,\ldots [7N/8-3E_{0}/4]-1\}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;else $//$缩短</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F},\mathrm{tmp}}=[E_{0}:N-1];$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;If $E\gt 128$</p>
<p>$\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm F,\mathrm{tmp}}^N=\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm F,\mathrm{tmp}}^N\cup\left\{\frac N4,\frac N2\right\}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{end}\mathrm{if}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>$\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm I,\mathrm{tmp}}^N=\boldsymbol Q_0^{N-1}\setminus\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm F,\mathrm{tmp}}^N;$</p>
<p>$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I},\mathrm{tmp}}$中$(T_{\mathrm{I}1})$个可靠性度量值最高的比特索引组成$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}1}$;$//\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}1}$是信息位</p>
<p>$\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm I,\mathrm{tmp}}^N=\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm I,\mathrm{tmp}}^N\setminus\left(\overline{\boldsymbol Q}_{\mathrm{I1}}^N\cup\left[0,1,2,\cdots,\frac N2-1\right]\right);$</p>
<p>$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I},\mathrm{tmp}}$中$(K+n_{\mathrm{PC}}-T_{\mathrm{I}1})$个可靠性度量值最高的比特索引组成$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}2}$;$//\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}2}$是信息位</p>
<p>$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}}=\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}1}\cup \overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}2}$;$//\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}}$ 是信息位</p>
<p>$\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F}}=\mathbf{Q}^{\mathrm{N}-1}_{0}\setminus \overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{I}}$ ;$//\overline{\mathbf{Q}}^{\mathrm{N}}_{\mathrm{F}}$是冻结位</p>

:::

(gbt-48336-201-2026-clause-6-2-6-1-3-2)=
#### 6.2.6.1.3.2 信道比特序列生成

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>记$N$ 为码块编码后的比特序列的长度$,E_{0}$为第一次传输时经过速率匹配后的信道比特序列长度$,E$ 为任意一次传输时经过速率匹配后的信道比特序列长度,初传时$E=E_{0}$;rv<sub>id</sub>为该次传输时采用的冗余版本号$(\mathrm{rv}_{\mathrm{id}}=0,1,2$或者3)。速率匹配的输出为序列$e_{k}$,$k=0$,1,$2,\ldots ,E-1$,根据以下方式产生,k0根据表12以及rv<sub>id</sub>的配置得到。$M$ 为循环buffer长度。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>if $K/E_{0}\gt 7/16$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$M=\mathrm{min}(E_{0},N)//$缩短和$\mathrm{CC}$重传</p>
<p>else</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$M=N//$打孔和$\mathrm{IR}$重传</p>
<p>$\;\text{end if}\;$</p>
<p>for $k=0$ to $E-1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$e_{\mathrm{k}}=d_{\mathrm{mod}((\mathrm{k}_{0}+\mathrm{k}),\mathrm{M})}$</p>
<p>$\;\text{end for}\;$</p>

:class: standard-paragraph

<p>输入$E$ 长度待交织序列$e$,输出$E$ 长度交织后序列$f$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>如果$E\gt E_{\mathrm{int}}$,输出序列$f$ 即为待交织序列$e$;否则采用以下方法获得交织后序列$f$。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>for $i=0$;$i\lt E$;$i=i+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{Rid}=i/\mathrm{Col}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{Cid}=\mathrm{mod}(i,\mathrm{Col})$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\mathrm{Qid}=\mathrm{mod}(\mathrm{Rid},I_{\mathrm{num}})$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$B_{\mathrm{tmp}}[\mathrm{Rid}\times \mathrm{Col}+\mathrm{Ise}q[\mathrm{Qid}][\mathrm{Cid}]]=e[\mathrm{Rid}\times \mathrm{Col}+\mathrm{Cid}]$</p>
<p>$\;\text{end for}\;$</p>
<p>$R_{\mathrm{use}}=[E/\mathrm{Col}]$</p>
<p>$f_{\mathrm{id}}=0$</p>
<p>for $i=0$;$i\lt 2$;$i=i+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;for $j=0$;$j\lt R_{\mathrm{use}}$;$j=j+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;for $k=0$;$k\lt \mathrm{Col}/2$;$k=k+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$B_{\mathrm{id}}=(j\times 2+i)\times \mathrm{Col}/2+k$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;if $B_{\mathrm{tmp}}[B_{\mathrm{id}}]!=\mathrm{NULL}$</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\mathbf{f}[f_{\mathrm{id}}]=B_{\mathrm{tmp}}[B_{\mathrm{id}}]$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$f_{\mathrm{id}}=f_{\mathrm{id}}+1$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end if}\;//\mathrm{ifB}_{\mathrm{tmp}}[B_{\mathrm{id}}]!=\mathrm{NULL}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end for}\;//k$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end for}\;//j$</p>
<p>$\;\text{end for}\;//i$</p>

### 6.2.6.1.4 码块级联

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>码块级联的输入比特序列是$f_{r(k)}$,其中$r=0,\ldots ,C-1$代表共$C$ 个码块$,k=0,\ldots ,E_{r}-1$,$E_{r}$ 是第$r$ 个码块速率匹配后的比特数量。码块级联的输出比特序列是$g_{k}$,其中$k=0,\ldots ,G-1$,$g_{k}$是由不同的码块比特序列$f_{r(k)}$按照码块顺序串行组合而成,其生成方法如下:</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-algorithm

<p>$k=0$;</p>
<p>$r=0$;</p>
<p>while $r\lt C$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$j=0$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;while $j\lt E_{r}$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$g_{k}=f_{r(j)}$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$k=k+1$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;&#8195;$j=j+1$;</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$\;\text{end while}\;$</p>
<p>&#8195;&#8195;$r=r+1$;</p>
<p>$\;\text{end while}\;$</p>

:::

<p>6.2.7 3<strong>D-FISA</strong><strong>流程</strong></p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>当有数据(包括业务数据和高层信令)传输需求时,G节点在时域以1个无线帧为粒度、频域以基础载波为粒度竞争信道。竞争信道成功后,以同步信息块发送周期为粒度,占用竞争到的每个基础载波,占用信道结束后 G 节点释放信道。当没有数据传输需求时,G 节点以无线帧为粒度处于空闲状态,不发送任何信号。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>G节点竞争信道成功后,在竞争到的一个或多个基础载波上,G 节点发送同步信息块和广播信息。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>在其中1个基础载波上发送同步信息块和广播信息的方式为:从竞争信道成功后的第一个无线帧开始,按照同步信息块和广播信息各自的周期为周期,在 G 节点释放信道之前周期发送同步信息块和广播信息。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>T节点通过盲检测 G节点发送的同步信息,确定自身连接的 G 节点占用的资源,并在自身连接的G节点占用的资源中,按照 G节点调度与 G节点通信。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-list-block

<ul class="standard-list">
<li><p>——— 步骤1:T 节点未盲检到自身连接的 G 节点的同步信息,则在每下一个无线帧再次盲检测G节点发送的同步信息,直到 T节点在至少一个基础载波上盲检自身连接的 G节点的同步信息成功并且校验 G节点广播信息成功,执行步骤2。</p>
</li>
<li><p>——— 步骤2:T节点在盲检自身连接的 G节点的同步信息成功的基础载波上,在1个同步信息块发送周期内,按照 G 节点的调度传输数据,并在下一个同步信息块 SAB发送周期再次盲检测G节点发送的同步信息。若 T节点在至少一个基础载波上盲检自身连接的 G 节点的同步信息成功,则再次执行步骤2;否则执行步骤1。</p>
</li></ul>
