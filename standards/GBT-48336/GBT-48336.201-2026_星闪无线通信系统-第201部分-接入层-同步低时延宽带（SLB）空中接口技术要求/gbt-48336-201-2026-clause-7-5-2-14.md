# 7.5.2.14 XRC释放消息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
XRCRelease ::= SEQUENCE {
    xrc-Identifier  XRC-Identifier,
    causeRelease    XRCReleaseReason    OPTIONAL, -- Need OR
    lateNonCriticalExtension    OCTET STRING    OPTIONAL,
```
