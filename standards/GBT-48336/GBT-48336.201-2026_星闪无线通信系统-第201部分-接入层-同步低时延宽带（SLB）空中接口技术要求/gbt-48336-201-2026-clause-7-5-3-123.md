# 7.5.3.123 超帧传输模式信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
SR-SuperFrameMode ::= ENUMERATED {continuous, discontinuous}
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>当该值为continuous时,指示超帧连续发送模式;当该值为discontinuous时,指示超帧不连续发送模式。</p>

:::
