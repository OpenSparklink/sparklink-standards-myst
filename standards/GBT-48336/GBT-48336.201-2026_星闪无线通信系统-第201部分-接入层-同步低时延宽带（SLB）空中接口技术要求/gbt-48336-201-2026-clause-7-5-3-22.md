# 7.5.3.22 频率同步调整量信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
FrequencySync-Value ::= INTEGER (-4800..4800)
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>频率调整信息的范围为$-480\mathrm{kHz}\sim 480\mathrm{kHz}$,反馈粒度为0.1kHz,负值代表频率降低,正值代表频率升高。</p>

:::
