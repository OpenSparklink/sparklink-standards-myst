# 7.5.3.90 半静态调度配置信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
SPS-Config ::= SEQUENCE {
    sps-ConfigTLToAddModList    SPS-ConfigTlinkList OPTIONAL,
    sps-ConfigGLToAddModList    SPS-ConfigGlinkList OPTIONAL,
    sps-ConfigTLToRelease   SPS-ConfigTlinkList OPTIONAL,
    sps-ConfigGLToRelease   SPS-ConfigGlinkList OPTIONAL,
    ...
}
SPS-ConfigGlinkList ::= SEQUENCE (SIZE (1..8)) OF SPS-ConfigGlink
SPS-ConfigTlinkList ::= SEQUENCE (SIZE (1..8)) OF SPS-ConfigTlink
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>当 G链路或 T链路半静态调度配置同时包含添加和删除操作时,T 节点宜先进行删除操作,再进行添加操作。</p>

:::
