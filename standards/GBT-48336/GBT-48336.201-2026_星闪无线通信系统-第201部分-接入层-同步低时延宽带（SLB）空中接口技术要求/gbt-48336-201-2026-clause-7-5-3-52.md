# 7.5.3.52 通用梳齿集合信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
CombInfo-0 ::= ENUMERATED {off, comb2, spare2, spare1}
CombInfo-1 ::= ENUMERATED {off, comb2, comb4, spare1}
CombInfo-2 ::= ENUMERATED {off, comb2, comb4, comb8}
CombInfo-3 ::= ENUMERATED {off, comb2, comb4, comb8, comb16}
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>针对某种类型信息或信号,当取值为off时,不采用梳齿发送;当取值comb2时,间隔1个子载波发送;当取值为comb4时,间隔3个子载波发送;当取值为comb8时,间隔7个子载波发送;当取值为comb16时,间隔15个子载波发送。</p>

:::
