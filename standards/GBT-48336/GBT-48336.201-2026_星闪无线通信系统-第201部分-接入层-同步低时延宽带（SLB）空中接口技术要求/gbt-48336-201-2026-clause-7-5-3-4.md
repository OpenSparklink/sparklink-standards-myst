# 7.5.3.4 载波信道号配置信息

:::{div}
:class: figure-code

```asn1
-- ASN1START
CarrierChannelListConf ::= SEQUENCE (SIZE (1..16)) OF ChannelNumber
-- ASN1STOP
```

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>CarrierChannelListConf指示同一个工作频段上的、一到多个载波分别对应的一到多个信道的信道号集合。</p>

:::
