## B.1 服务示例

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>服务及其服务成员的示例如表B.1所示,示例中的服务与服务成员与标准定义服务与服务成员无关联关系。</p>

:class: standard-paragraph

:::

## B.2 服务结构发现示例

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>按B.1的服务示例为参考,如客户端发送SSAP_FIND_STRUCTURE_REQ 消息并指示查找类型为服务结构,且句柄范围设置为0x0001~0xFFFF,则服务器将返回以下内容。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>BatteryService服务结构:{</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>{0x0010,0x00,BatteryService,{0x00}},{0x0013,0x02,BatteryLevel,0x00,{0x01,0x02}}}ThermometerHumidityService服务结构:{{0x0100,0x00,ThermometerHumidityService,{0x00}},{0x0101,0x05,ManufacturerService,0x0500,0x0503},{0x0102,0x0D,VendorSpecificServicexx ,0x0550,0x0568},{0x0103,0x02,Temperature,0x00,{0x02,0x04,0x01}},{0x0104,0x02,RelativeHumidity,0x00,{0x02,0x04,0x01}},}SmokeAlarmService服务结构:{{0x0200,0x00,SmokeAlarmService,{0x00}},{0x0201,0x04,SmokeAlarm Event,0x00,{0x00}}}PositionService服务结构:{{0x0300,0x00,PositionService,{0x00}},{0x0301,0x04,LatitudeLongitud,0x00,{0x00}}{0x0302,0x03,MoveMethod,0x00,{0x00}}}ManufacturerService服务结构:{{0x0500,0x01,ManufacturerService,{0x00}},{0x0501,0x04,ManufacturerName,0x00,{0x00}}{0x0502,0x03,SerialNumber,0x00,{0x00}}}VendorSpecificServicexxx 服务结构:{{0x0550,0x09,VendorSpecificServicexxx ,{0x00}},{0x0568,0x0A,VendorSpecificType,0x00,{0x00}}}数据内容超过SSAP_FIND_STRUCTURE_RSP消息单次承载时,可选择分包多次发送。</p>

:::
