## 9.2.2.2 逻辑信道建立请求

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>AccessLinkEstablish.request原语用于连接管理功能单元向接入层申请为一条传输通道建立接入层逻辑信道(见表97)。接入层可选择创建一条新的逻辑信道用于承载该传输通道数据,也可复用一条已建立的逻辑信道承载该传输通道数据,具体留给接入层实现。</p>

:::

:::{div}
:class: standard-paragraph

<p>AccessLinkEstablish.request(Version,LocalIndex,SrcTCID,TargetL2ID,ExpectedTransportMode,(optional)ExpectedLogicalChannelMode,(optional)ExpectedDirection,(optional)XQI,(optional)--onlyforSLB AID,(optional)ExclusiveIndication,(optional)OtherDedicatedAccsessLayerParams,(optional)Extension)</p>
